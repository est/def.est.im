run
/rʌn/
verb · noun · A1
核心：移动；持续运作。十义由此派生。

释义

  1. 1. to move quickly using your legs 跑；奔跑
    She runs five miles every morning. 她每天早上跑五英里。
  2. 2. to manage, control, or operate 经营；运行（机器）
    She runs a successful software company. 她经营着一家成功的软件公司。
  3. 3. (of liquid) to flow 液体流淌
    The water runs freely from the tap. 水龙头里的水哗哗地流着。
  4. 4. to stretch or extend in a direction 延伸；通向
    The road runs along the coast. 这条路沿海岸线延伸。
  5. 5. to be a candidate in an election 竞选
    She is running for mayor this fall. 她今年秋天竞选市长。
  6. 6. (of color) to bleed in fabric 褪色；混色
    The red ran into the white in the wash. 洗的时候红色渗进了白色。

名词

  1. 7. an act or period of running 跑步；赛跑
    He went for a run before breakfast. 他早饭前去跑了步。
  2. 8. a continuous series 一连串；一段时期
    We had a run of bad luck. 我们接连走霉运。
  3. 9. a small stream 小溪
    A run crossed the field behind the house. 一条小溪从屋后的田里流过。

词源

源自古英语 rinnan，与古诺斯语 rinna 同源，可追溯到原始印欧语 *re-「流动」。run 天生就带「一直流动」的底色——上面的流动义不是比喻，是它本来的血统。
